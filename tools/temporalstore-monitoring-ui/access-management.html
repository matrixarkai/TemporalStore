OpenViking/VikingMem alignment

            
Access management maps directly to agent-context runtime roles.

          

        

        

          
OpenViking-style contextmemory, resources, and skills are organized under account/tenant/user/session boundaries.

          
VikingMem-style extractionsession windows and batch extraction get separate keys from online retrieval hooks.

          
Event/entity memoryagent hooks can ingest raw turns, final answers, confirmations, and feedback without exposing internal schemas.

          
Temporal governanceadmins can disable tenant, user, or key while keeping historical context replayable.
