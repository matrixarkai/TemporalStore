Product Thesis

One platform for the three online-state problems teams keep rebuilding.

Real-time products usually stitch together streams, caches, online stores, feature services, and metadata databases. MatrixArk turns that sprawl into three focused products that share deployment, observability, and operations.

01

TemporalStore

TemporalStore is a temporal feature serving engine for decisions that depend on what just happened: rolling windows, filtered aggregates, distinct counts, long sequences, frequency caps, and structured AI context.

  • Launch new risk, ads, recommendation, and agent policies without a new stream job for every window.
  • Keep temporal computation in the serving layer, close to the entity state being queried.
  • Serve both fresh answers and replayable persisted state, not only latest-value lookups.
02

MatrixDB

MatrixDB is for multi-tenant, high-volume online data that should stay simple: user profiles, item attributes, product state, service state, and cache-plus-storage workloads.

  • Give many teams and tenants a familiar access path while gaining a managed serving plane.
  • Keep plain KV and profile reads simple instead of forcing every workload into temporal semantics.
  • Scale customer-facing serving where tenant isolation, capacity, rollout, and visibility matter.
03

MatrixKV

MatrixKV is transactional KV for state that must be correct: metadata, leases, routing records, table state, timestamps, and control-plane coordination.

  • Protect ownership and routing with strong consistency rather than cache freshness assumptions.
  • Give failover, placement, and table operations a clear consistency boundary.
  • Support TemporalStore and MatrixDB with a dedicated control-plane foundation.
