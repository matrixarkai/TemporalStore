Product Thesis

One platform for the three online-state problems teams keep rebuilding.

Real-time products usually stitch together streams, caches, online stores, feature services, and metadata databases. MatrixArk turns that sprawl into three focused products that share deployment, observability, and operations while staying honest about which engine owns which state.

01

TemporalStore

TemporalStore is the temporal decision-state engine: rolling windows, filtered aggregates, distinct counts, long behavior sequences, frequency caps, and structured AI or agent context are stored and queried as first-class online models.

  • Launch new risk, ads, recommendation, and agent policies without a new stream job for every window.
  • Keep temporal computation in the serving layer, close to the entity state being queried.
  • Serve both fresh answers and replayable persisted state, not only latest-value lookups.
02

MatrixDB

MatrixDB is the performant KV database for serving and offline access: user profiles, item attributes, account state, hashes, strings, service state, cache-plus-storage workloads, scans, exports, and nearline query paths that need familiar access and managed capacity.

  • Give many teams and tenants a familiar access path while gaining a managed serving and query plane.
  • Keep plain KV and profile reads simple while persisted data remains available for offline jobs.
  • Scale customer-facing serving and offline-queryable state where tenant isolation, capacity, rollout, and visibility matter.
03

MatrixKV

MatrixKV is the strongly consistent transactional KV engine: metadata, leases, routing records, table state, timestamps, ownership, and coordination records that cannot be treated like ordinary cache entries.

  • Protect ownership and routing with strong consistency rather than cache freshness assumptions.
  • Give failover, placement, and table operations a clear consistency boundary.
  • Support TemporalStore and MatrixDB with a dedicated control-plane foundation.
